How I Designed an Enterprise Network in Cisco Packet Tracer

Oct 2026 · Networking

For my networking project I built a full enterprise network with 6 VLANs. The biggest lesson: plan your IP addressing first (192.168.10.0/24 to 192.168.60.0/24), then configure trunks, SVIs and DHCP in order. ACLs taught me how real companies protect sensitive departments like Finance — IT can access Finance, but Sales cannot. Simulation mode in Packet Tracer made packet flow finally click for me.
